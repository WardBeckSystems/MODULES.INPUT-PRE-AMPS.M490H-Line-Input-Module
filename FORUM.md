## big m490h racking project.

_WBS RACKMOUNTING · Oct 23, 2010|43_

> There was a Left / Right assign 
>
> It's strange: the M490H was a mono unit. When you pushed P1 it went to Program 1 LEFT and RIGHT stereo bus. P2 went to a different Left Right 
>
> SO, to use the P1 and P2 buttons as stereo assigns you need to take P1 Left and P2 Right output. you could add a pan knob, but it's a really expensive pot...
